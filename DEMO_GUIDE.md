# 3–5 Minute Project Demo Guide

## Opening
“Bharat Sarvodaya Engine is a community opportunity decision-support prototype. It connects a community's problem, existing resources, local skills, and demand to possible inclusive livelihood or service ideas.”

## Live walkthrough

1. **Input:** Use the sample scenario, or enter a community name, problem, resources, skills, and demand.
2. **Opportunity results:** Explain that the engine uses transparent keyword/rule matching to suggest relevant opportunity concepts.
3. **Readiness indicator:** Show demand fit (40%), resource fit (35%), and skill fit (25%). Explain that this is a prototype indicator, not a proven prediction.
4. **Impact simulator:** Move the workforce, demand, and resource sliders. Explain that the visualisation changes as assumptions change.
5. **History and comparison:** Generate a second scenario. Select two different saved scenarios in the comparison section and compare their inputs and readiness indicators.
6. **Report:** Generate the printable report and choose “Save as PDF” in the browser print dialog.

## Likely judge questions

**Is this a real AI prediction model?**  
Not in this version. The current opportunity matching is rule/keyword-based, and the readiness calculation is transparent. This makes the prototype easy to inspect and improve with validated field data later.

**Are the costs and job numbers guaranteed?**  
No. They are illustrative placeholders for demonstrating the concept. Real estimates require local surveys, supplier quotes, and stakeholder validation.

**How does it benefit communities?**  
It provides a structured starting point for connecting local needs with available resources and skills, while encouraging small pilots and validation before investment.

**What would be the next development stage?**  
Collect consent-based community data, validate the scoring with domain experts, add local-language support, and evaluate suggestions through small pilots with measurable outcomes.
